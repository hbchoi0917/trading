- **Wheel strategy** — CSP → assignment → covered call rotation on high-premium tickers

## Key Lessons Learned

**Covered calls:**
- Holding through a rally and selling stock above strike outperforms early BTC in most cases
- Rolling short calls forward at the same strike generates ongoing premium recovery

**Put spreads:**
- Single-name idiosyncratic drops require faster stop-loss than sector-wide moves
- Wider OTM strikes and reduced size on high-volatility names improves risk-adjusted return

**Wheel strategy:**
- CSP → assignment → covered call cycle validated as net positive on suitable tickers

---

## Risk Management Rules

1. Max risk per spread: capped per position
2. DTE discipline: open 28–45 DTE; close at 80% profit or 12 DTE
3. Drawdown circuit breaker: pause new positions if monthly loss threshold hit
4. High-beta names: limited contract count; quantum computing sector excluded
5. Call selling: only when underlying is below 52-week midpoint
